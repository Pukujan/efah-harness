# FINDING-003 — `tier: frontier` labels do not match the models they label

**Raised:** 2026-08-02 by the owner, during the Q1 discussion
**Class:** owner decision required (alias map is owner data, authority level 1–2)
**Status:** OPEN — nothing changed. Recorded for owner adjudication.

## What the owner spotted

The builder cited the assurance roles as "frontier" models. That was repeating
`model-policy.yaml`'s labels rather than checking them. The owner's push-back was
correct.

`claude-opus-5` recommends **`claude-opus-5-thinking`** ("flat $0.048, same
generation, and it accepts `reasoning_effort`") as the frontier Anthropic choice
— and it is live on the eval gateway but assigned to no role.

## Why this is material, not cosmetic

`model-policy.yaml` states the intent directly: *"Tiering per owner decision
2026-08-01: frontier for assurance, mid for bulk. Assurance is where a wrong
answer costs the most, so capability goes there."*

For at least three assurance roles the mapping does not deliver that intent. The
sharpest case is `mutant_author`:

> §17.4 requires a trusted oracle to name **the mutants it kills**. The mutation
> gate currently reports **25 mutants, 25 killed, kill rate 1.0**. A kill rate of
> 1.0 is only meaningful if the mutants are hard. Mutants authored by a fast-tier
> model are plausibly easy ones, and an easy mutant suite that is fully killed
> **manufactures confidence** rather than measuring it.

The same argument applies to `sealed_holdout_author` under DEC-006: holdouts
about to be generated by a model are only worth the capability behind them.

## Not changed, and why

The alias map is owner data (contract §1.2 authority 1–2). §0 forbids the builder
reinterpreting the selected architecture, and silently upgrading a model the
owner tiered would be exactly that — even in the "safe" direction. Cost is also
an owner fact: `grok-4.5` is flat $0.004/call while some alternatives are
per-token, and the owner's `prohibited_models` list shows cost trapping is a live
concern for them.

## Options for the owner

- **A — leave as declared.** Zero cost change. Accept that the mutation kill rate
  is weaker evidence than it appears, and record that in honest debt.
- **B — upgrade the three that contradict their label**, using models already
  live on the eval gateway: `mutant_author → gemini-3.1-pro-preview` (stays
  Google, preserves family separation from openai/implementer);
  `contract_compliance_auditor → claude-opus-5-thinking`; `release_verifier →`
  keep `glm-5-turbo` or move if a stronger zhipu route appears. Family
  separation is preserved in every case, so no `role_incompatibilities` rule
  changes.
- **C — upgrade `mutant_author` only.** The narrowest fix: it is the role whose
  weakness most directly inflates a gate result.

**Recommendation: C, then B if the spend is acceptable.** Confidence: medium-high
that the mutant tier matters; medium on the other two, where the label is wrong
but the consequence is smaller.

The owner can answer this through the control surface — which is what §11.7
exists for, and avoids opening a second question round that
`autonomy-policy.yaml` forbids.
