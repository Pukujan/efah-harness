## 5. The principle that generalised

> **Any checker whose expected value is a constant the candidate knows is forgeable. The
> expected value must carry per-run entropy the candidate cannot predict — and travel on a
> channel the candidate cannot write.**

Two illustrations worth keeping:

- **`command_evidence_check`**: appending one backticked pytest command flipped **18 of 20
  rows** FAIL→PASS. Fixed to ABSTAIN. Kappa moved 0.1333 → 0.0 — *the published 0.1333 had
  been purchased by that single forgeable PASS.*
- **`manifest.json`** had 7 of 8 stale pins, one matching no committed state that has ever
  existed. The manifest has exactly one commit in its entire history.

And the pattern behind the pattern: **detection works; closing does not.** The promotion bug
was found three times across four weeks with the correct fix written down each time. The
cwe/crypto forgery was filed three weeks ago and *credited as fixed*.

  supports `--resume`; detach with `setsid`.

  duplicate that has already caused one wrong answer.

  warning into every prompt that reads them.
